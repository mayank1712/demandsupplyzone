Replays the last ~3 years of candles one bar at a time and follows the scanner's rules, using only data known at each moment: daily action → weekly curve → limit order at the LTF zone's proximal line, SL at distal, target 1:R:R. One trade per stock at a time and one trade per zone. If a candle touches both SL and target, the SL counts. Results are in R (1R = the amount risked per trade).

Every week the app saves the 2h zones formed that week (and the trade setup at Friday's close) with frozen entry, stop loss and 1:5 target, then checks them every following week: filled? target or stop? Both stop rules are followed: touch and closing basis. Untouched zones expire after 8 weeks. Runs automatically every Saturday with weekly.bat (Windows Task Scheduler), or with the button above.
